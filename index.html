How to play

Choose one of the three shapes, then tap a spot on the 10×10 board. Complete a full row or column to clear it and earn points. The game ends when no available shape fits.

  • Clear multiple lines for bigger scores.
  • Use the three-piece tray strategically.
  • Your best score is saved on this device when browser storage is available.

Tip: for offline play, download the ZIP, extract it, and open index.html in a modern browser. The game itself does not need internet access.
